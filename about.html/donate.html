Donate to Support Our Cause

Your Donation Saves Lives

As a non-profit organization, we rely entirely on the generosity of the public to continue our work of resuce and rehabilitation and to find forever loving homes for the animals. Every donation, big or small will help us provide food, shelter, medical care, and love to our animals .

Your support enables us to rescue animals from neglect and abuse, rehabilitate them, and find them loving forever homes. Together, we can make a difference in the lives of countless animals.

How Your Donation Helps

R50

Provides food for a rescued animal for one week

R150

Covers vaccinations for one animal

R300

Provides a spay/neuter surgery for one animal

R500

Covers emergency medical treatment for an injured animal
