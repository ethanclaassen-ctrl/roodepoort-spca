Volunteer With Us

Make a Difference in Animals' Lives

Volunteers are the most important members of our organization. Without your commitment and passion, we wouldn't be able to provide the level of care that our animals deserve. Join our team to help make a difference in the lives of every rescued animal at our premesis.

Volunteer Opportunities

Animal Care

Help with feeding, grooming, and exercising, and rehabilitating animals. Assist with cleaning enclosures.

Adoption Assistant

Help potential adopters find their perfect pet. Assist with adoption paperwork and follow-up calls.

Events & Fundraising

Assist with organizing and running events, fundraisers, and educational programs.

Administrative Support

Help with office tasks, data entry, answering phones, filing, and applications.

Requirements

  • Must be 16 years or older (some positions require 18+)
  • Complete a volunteer application form
  • Attend an orientation session
  • Commit to a regular schedule (minimum 4 hours per month)
  • Have a genuine love for animals and their welfare
  • Be reliable and responsible

Benefits of Volunteering

  • Make a positive difference in animals' lives
  • Gain valuable experience working with animals
  • Develop new skills and enhance existing ones
  • Meet like-minded people and make new friends
  • Improve your mental and physical wellbeing
  • Receive ongoing training and support
