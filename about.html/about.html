About Us

The SPCA is dedicated to preventing cruelty to animals, promoting responsible pet ownership, and protecting vulnerable animals in our communities. We provide shelter, medical care, and adoption opportunities for thousands of animals each year.

Our Mission

Rescue

We rescue animals in distress and provide them with safety, love, and care.

Protect

We protect animals from neglect and abuse through inspections and legal action.

Educate

We educate communities on the importance of kindness and responsible ownership.
