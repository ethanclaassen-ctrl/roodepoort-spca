Reporting Emergencies

If you are reporting an animal emergency or cruelty case, please call our emergency line immediately at (011) 123-4568. Our emergency line is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

When reporting an emergency, please provide as much information as possible, including:

• Your name and contact number
• The exact location of the animal
• A description of the situation
• The condition of the animal
• Any other relevant details
