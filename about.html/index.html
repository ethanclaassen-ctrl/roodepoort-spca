About Our Organization And People Who Work Here

The Roodepoort SPCA was founded in 1945 and is one of the oldest SPCAs in the country. We are here to help all animals that suffer from abuse and neglect. We work towards preventing animal cruelty and follow strict adoption processes as well as sterilizing, vaccinating, and deworming all our rescued animals before adopting them out. Our funds come from donations and big corporates as we are not funded by the government.
